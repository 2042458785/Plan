# 店铺自动回复与快捷回复（中文、英文、马来语）

## 推荐设置一：首次欢迎自动回复

这是最适合直接放入Shopee自动回复的双语版本：

> Hi, welcome to NELORA HOME! Thank you for contacting us. Please leave your question and we will reply as soon as possible. For product size, material and colour, please check the product description first. Thank you!
>
> Hi, selamat datang ke NELORA HOME! Terima kasih kerana menghubungi kami. Sila tinggalkan pertanyaan anda dan kami akan membalas secepat mungkin. Untuk saiz, bahan dan warna produk, sila semak penerangan produk terlebih dahulu. Terima kasih!

中文释义：

你好，欢迎来到 NELORA HOME！感谢你的咨询。请留下你的问题，我们会尽快回复。关于商品尺寸、材质和颜色，请先查看商品详情，谢谢！

## 推荐设置二：非工作时间自动回复

> Hi, thank you for contacting NELORA HOME. We are currently away, but your message has been received. We will reply as soon as we are available. Please refer to the product description and Shopee’s estimated delivery date while waiting. Thank you for your patience!
>
> Hi, terima kasih kerana menghubungi NELORA HOME. Kami sedang tiada buat sementara waktu tetapi mesej anda telah diterima. Kami akan membalas sebaik sahaja tersedia. Sementara menunggu, sila rujuk penerangan produk dan tarikh anggaran penghantaran Shopee. Terima kasih atas kesabaran anda!

## 推荐设置三：咨询量较大时自动回复

> Hi, thank you for your message. We are currently receiving a high number of enquiries and may take a little longer to reply. Please do not send repeated messages, as we will respond in order as soon as possible. Thank you for your understanding!
>
> Hi, terima kasih atas mesej anda. Kami sedang menerima banyak pertanyaan dan mungkin memerlukan sedikit masa untuk membalas. Sila elakkan menghantar mesej berulang kerana kami akan membalas mengikut giliran secepat mungkin. Terima kasih atas pemahaman anda!

## 快捷回复01：有货

### English

Yes, the option currently shown as available can be ordered. We recommend placing the order while stock is still available.

### Bahasa Melayu

Ya, pilihan yang sedang dipaparkan sebagai tersedia boleh dipesan. Kami mencadangkan anda membuat pesanan selagi stok masih ada.

## 快捷回复02：查看尺寸

### English

Please check the product description and size image for detailed measurements. Manual measurement may have a small difference.

### Bahasa Melayu

Sila semak penerangan produk dan gambar saiz untuk ukuran terperinci. Ukuran manual mungkin mempunyai sedikit perbezaan.

## 快捷回复03：跨境发货

### English

This item is shipped from overseas through a Shopee-supported logistics channel. Please refer to Shopee’s estimated delivery date before ordering.

### Bahasa Melayu

Produk ini dihantar dari luar negara melalui saluran logistik yang disokong Shopee. Sila rujuk tarikh anggaran penghantaran Shopee sebelum membuat pesanan.

## 快捷回复04：已经发货

### English

Your parcel has been shipped. Please check the latest tracking information on your Shopee order page.

### Bahasa Melayu

Bungkusan anda telah dihantar. Sila semak maklumat penjejakan terkini pada halaman pesanan Shopee.

## 快捷回复05：物流暂未更新

### English

Cross-border tracking may take some time to update between logistics facilities. Please allow a little more time and continue checking the Shopee order page.

### Bahasa Melayu

Penjejakan rentas sempadan mungkin mengambil sedikit masa untuk dikemas kini antara pusat logistik. Sila tunggu sedikit lagi dan terus semak halaman pesanan Shopee.

## 快捷回复06：破损取证

### English

We’re sorry about the issue. Please send clear photos of the outer packaging, shipping label, damaged area and all items received. Please keep the packaging while we assist you.

### Bahasa Melayu

Kami memohon maaf atas masalah tersebut. Sila hantar gambar jelas pembungkusan luar, label penghantaran, bahagian yang rosak dan semua barang yang diterima. Sila simpan pembungkusan sementara kami membantu anda.

## 快捷回复07：退货退款

### English

Please open the order in Shopee and select Return/Refund. Choose the correct reason and upload clear supporting photos or video so Shopee can record and process the request.

### Bahasa Melayu

Sila buka pesanan di Shopee dan pilih Return/Refund. Pilih sebab yang betul dan muat naik gambar atau video sokongan yang jelas supaya Shopee dapat merekod dan memproses permintaan tersebut.

## 快捷回复08：感谢下单

### English

Thank you for your order with NELORA HOME! We will process it as soon as possible within Shopee’s required timeline.

### Bahasa Melayu

Terima kasih kerana membuat pesanan dengan NELORA HOME! Kami akan memprosesnya secepat mungkin dalam tempoh yang ditetapkan oleh Shopee.
